humidity changes sign at CDD = 6.9, that is Tmin = 24.9 C:
  Tmin 22 C: +10 points of RH moves P(nocturnal) by -1.6 points
  Tmin 24 C: +10 points of RH moves P(nocturnal) by -1.1 points
  Tmin 28 C: +10 points of RH moves P(nocturnal) by +4.4 points
  Tmin 32 C: +10 points of RH moves P(nocturnal) by +10.1 points

  mild and humid = cloudy, which damps the afternoon too
  hot and humid  = latent load, the compressor cannot stop. Monsoon nights.
